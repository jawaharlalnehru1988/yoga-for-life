Bhujangasana

Cobra Pose

A powerful backbend that awakens the serpent power (Kundalini) and opens the heart center. Derived from the Sanskrit words 'Bhujanga' meaning cobra and 'Asana' meaning posture.

Strengthening Heart Opening Intermediate
Vedic Wisdom
format_list_numbered

Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Foundation
Preparation

Lie prone on the mat with your legs extended behind you, tops of the feet resting on the floor. Keep your feet together or hip-distance apart.

air Breath: Natural Awareness
2
Hand Placement

Spread your hands on the floor under your shoulders. Hug your elbows into your body. Press the tops of the feet and thighs firmly into the floor.

air Breath: Deep Exhale
3
The Ascension
Critical Step

On an inhalation, begin to straighten the arms to lift the chest off the floor, going only to the height at which you can maintain a connection through your pubis to the floor.

arrow_upward Breath: Slow Inhale
4
The Hold

Distribute the backbend evenly through the entire spine. Gaze forward or slightly upward without crunching the back of the neck.

pause_circle Breath: Kumbhaka (Hold)
warning

Important: What Not To Do

  • cancel Don't over-straighten elbows or "lock" joints.
  • cancel Avoid shrugging shoulders up toward ears.
  • cancel Don't force the lift using only arm strength.
  • cancel Never hold your breath during the transition.
